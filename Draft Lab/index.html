Fast counter rules

Think in terms of the threat you need to answer

↗
Into Tanks

Prefer high sustained damage, percentage damage, knockback or slows. Keep enough space to kite.

✦
Into Assassins

Look for burst, anti-dive control, a defensive gadget or teammates who can peel.

◎
Into Artillery

Use mobility, walls you can break, long range or a flank that forces the thrower to reposition.
